# Network Focus brief: grading rubric

Score each dimension 1–5 using the spreadsheet rows (roster.json) as the only source of truth.

| Dimension | 5 | 3 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Grounding** | Every reason, risk and number traces to the person's row. Nothing invented. | Mostly grounded; one stretch or vague claim. | Claims that aren't in the data. |
| **Move specificity** | Each move is one concrete action this week: a channel, a specific ask, and why it fits this person. | Some moves are generic ("reach out", "reconnect"). | Moves are vague or interchangeable between people. |
| **Goal coverage** | Every goal has a credible pick, or the gap is explained. People serving two goals are recognized. | One goal is thin or unexplained. | A goal is ignored. |
| **Prioritization** | Picks are the highest-leverage people: open loops, time-sensitive windows, strong-but-quiet relationships. Decoys (strong but irrelevant, famous but cold, "not now") are left out. Timing requests are respected. | One questionable pick or one obvious miss. | Picks look like recency or strength sorting. |
| **Plain language** | A busy CEO can act on it in five minutes. No jargon, no hedging, one idea per sentence. | Some clutter or jargon. | Hard to act on. |

**Pass:** Grounding ≥ 4 and Move specificity ≥ 4.
